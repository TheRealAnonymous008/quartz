* Within category theory, we adopt the notion that [[Relation|functions]] are not just rules (mappings), but rules with a domain and codomain.
* Let $S,T$ be sets.  $\text{Hom}(X,Y)$ denotes the set of all functions with domain $X$ and codomain $Y$. 
	* Let $f:Y\to V$ be a function. The function $\text{Hom}(X,f) : \text{Hom}(X,Y)\to \text{Hom}(X,V)$ Is defined by the mapping  $g\mapsto f\circ g$.
	* Let  $h: W\to X$ be a function. The function $\text{Hom}(h,Y): \text{Hom}(X,Y) \to \text{Hom}(W,Y)$n is defined by the mapping $g\mapsto g\circ h$. 
	* Both of the above are called the **hom functors**.
