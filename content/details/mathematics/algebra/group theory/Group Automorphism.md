
* An **automorphism** is an [[Group Isomorphism|isomorphism]] of a [[Fundamental Constructs of Group Theory|group]] to itself (i.e., $\phi: G\to G$)
	* The **inner automorphism** of $G$ by $g$ is defined as 
	  $$
	  i_g(x)=gxg^{-1}
	  $$
	  Performing it is called the **conjugation** of $x$ by $g$
	*  A **conjugate subgroup** of $H$ is given by
	  $$
	  K=i_g(H)
	  $$
