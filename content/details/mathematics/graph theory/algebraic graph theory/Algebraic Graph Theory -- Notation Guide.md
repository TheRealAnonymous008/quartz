* We borrow from [[Graph Theory -- Notation Guide]] and [[Group Theory -- Notation Guide]].

* $\text{Sym}(n)$ - the symmetric group on a set of $n$ elements.
* $\text{Sym}(A)$ - the symmetric group on set $A$.
* $X^g$ - the graph obtained by applying the automorphism $g$.
* $\text{Aut}(X)$ - the automorphism group of graph $X$.

*****
* $\text{head}(\alpha)$ - head of arc $\alpha$
* $\text{tail}(\alpha)$ - tail of arc $\alpha$
* $X^{(s)}$ - the directed graph with $s$-arcs of $X$ as its vertices such that $(\alpha,\beta)$ is an arc if and only if $\alpha$ can be shunted onto $\beta$.
***** 
* $I$ - incidence (relation).
* $\mathcal{I}=(\mathcal P, \mathcal L, I)$ - incidence structure on points $\mathcal P$ , lines $\mathcal L$ and incidence relation $I$.
* $X(\mathcal{I})$ - the incidence / Levi graph of an
* $\text{PG}(2,q)$ - projective field plane of a vector space $V\subseteq \mathbb{F}^3$ where $\text{ord}(\mathbb{F})=q$. 
* $\text{PG}(3,q)$ - projective field plane of a vector space $V\subseteq \mathbb{F}^4$ where $\text{ord}(\mathbb{F})=q$.
