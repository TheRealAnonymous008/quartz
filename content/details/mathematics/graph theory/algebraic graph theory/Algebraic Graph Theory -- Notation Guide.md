* We borrow from [[Graph Theory -- Notation Guide]] and [[Group Theory -- Notation Guide]].

* $\text{Sym}(n)$ - the symmetric group on a set of $n$ elements.
* $\text{Sym}(A)$ - the symmetric group on set $A$.
* $X^g$ - the graph obtained by applying the automorphism $g$.

*****
* $\text{head}(\alpha)$ - head of arc $\alpha$
* $\text{tail}(\alpha)$ - tail of arc $\alpha$
* $X^{(s)}$ - the directed graph with $s$-arcs of $X$ as its vertices such that $(\alpha,\beta)$ is an arc if and only if $\alpha$ can be shunted onto $\beta$.
