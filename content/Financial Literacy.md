* *Live Below Your Means* - have money left over at the end of the month.  Save your money. 
* *Establish a Budget* to be informed of all financial decisions and their outcomes. 
* *Avoid Bad Debt* - any debt with a high interest rate should be paid off first.
* *Save* 
* *Invest Early, Often, and Diversely* - have wealth not just be in money but also in assets and equities. Also don't put your eggs in one basket. 
* *Maintain Discipline* - make a plan and stick to it.
